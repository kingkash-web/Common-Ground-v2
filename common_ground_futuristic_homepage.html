Should every student learn a digital skill before graduation?

Universities teach deep subject knowledge. The job market also values practical digital skills. How should students balance both?

🟢 YES, IT SHOULD BE EXPECTED

Digital skills open doors to jobs, business, and independent learning.

🟣 NOT FOR EVERYONE

Students need time for their main course, and not every skill fits every career.

Shared agreement progress62%

🤝 PROPOSED COMMON GROUND

Students should focus on their main studies while having opportunities to learn practical digital skills that fit their goals.
